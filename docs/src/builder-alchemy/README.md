# Builder Alchemy (Sister Framework)

> **Structured prompt systems for building products, careers, brands, and businesses.**

---

## 📌 Framework Overview

While **Hackathon Alchemy** is optimized for high-speed 24–48 hour prototype sprints, **Builder Alchemy** expands the prompt framework methodology to the complete lifecycle of product creation, company building, career progression, and brand development.

---

## 📁 Core Pillars in Development

### 1. 🚀 Startup & Product
- **MVP Building:** Validating customer demand before writing code.
- **SaaS Product Launch:** Pricing strategy, product positioning, and Go-To-Market execution.
- **Startup Fundraising:** Pitch deck structures, investor FAQs, and data room checklists.
- **Micro-SaaS:** Niche software businesses built by solo founders.
- **Product Management:** Feature prioritization, user interview synthesis, and roadmapping.

### 2. 💼 Career & Personal Branding
- **Job Application System:** ATS-optimized resumes, tailored cover letters, and outreach cadences.
- **Freelancing Business:** Proposal templates, client onboarding, and contract checklists.
- **Personal Branding:** Positioning, content pillars, and profile optimization.
- **Interview Preparation:** Technical deep-dives, system design walkthroughs, and behavioral scripts.

### 3. 🎨 Content & Creator Economy
- **YouTube Channel Launch:** Video scripting, thumbnail ideation, and distribution strategy.
- **Blog & Newsletter:** Editorial calendars, SEO-driven article blueprints, and retention mechanics.
- **Course Creation:** Curriculum design, exercise builders, and student outcome tracking.

### 4. ⚙️ Business Operations
- **Legal & Compliance:** Privacy policies, terms of service, and entity setup checklists.
- **Finance & Accounting:** P&L projections, runway calculations, and unit economics.
- **Sales & CRM:** Outbound email sequences, objection handling scripts, and CRM pipelines.
